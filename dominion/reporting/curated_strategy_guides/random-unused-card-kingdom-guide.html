Use two players, the normal basic supply, and a Potion pile. Each player starts with six Coppers, one Pasture, and three Estates. Shepherd’s Heirloom setup follows the Nocturne rules; Ally and rotating-pile setup follows the Allies rules.

The recommendation: Shepherd and Embassy money

Buy one Shepherd and one Embassy, with up to one Bauble; use them to support Gold and Province buying. This policy placed first in the eight-strategy finalist round robin, scoring 54.4% with ties counted as half a win across 1,400 games. The closest rival scored 53.5%, so this is a competitive recommendation, not a uniquely established optimum.

Opening and buying

  1. On a $4/$3 opening, buy Shepherd and Silver. On $5/$2, buy Embassy and Bauble. The same choices apply when the two hands arrive in the opposite order.
  2. Complete the small action package. Buy the first Embassy at $5 and the first Shepherd at $4. Keep one of each; one Bauble is a useful $2 purchase when you cannot afford Silver.
  3. Take Provinces whenever you reach $8. Otherwise favor Gold at $6–$7 and Silver at $3–$5 once the two action cards are acquired. Spend remaining money with Bauble’s extra buy when a worthwhile purchase is available.
  4. Switch to endgame points. With three or fewer Provinces left, Duchy takes priority over further deck building. With one Province left, Estate also takes priority over treasure. Pasture gives every Estate an additional point while you retain it.

Playing the deck

Play Shepherd before Embassy, discarding Victory cards to draw replacements. Shepherd preserves your action, so you can then play Embassy. Embassy draws five cards and discards three; the simulator discards Curses, non-action Victory cards, and Coppers before more useful treasure or actions.

Bauble normally supplies a coin and another buy. The policy requests a Favor only if its supply of Favors has run out. Woodworkers’ Guild can exchange an unplayed terminal action at the start of the Buy phase, but this small deck rarely needs that option.

The strategy buys no additional engine components. The large University, Counting House, Fairgrounds-diversity, and Forts plans tested did not displace the winning money policy. The result applies to these implemented policies on this fixed two-player kingdom.

How the board and strategy were selected

The starting catalog contained 95 registered strategies and 453 cards without explicit strategy-priority or Way-rule references. The draw excluded basic cards, Loot, Heirlooms, non-buyable special cards, and lower members of split piles. The resulting pool contained 359 names, sorted before sampling.

The recorded seed is 3496985294. The draw record preserves the eligible list, selected cards, Ally, and baseline commit. Re-running the same ten-card sample against this frozen list reproduces the board even though the live usage report has changed.

“Unused” means no explicit references in the catalog before this work. It does not establish that a card was absent from custom decision code, automatic gains, or written guides.

Rules corrected before testing

Cursed Village previously gave a Hex whenever played. It now gives the gainer a Hex when gained; playing it only grants actions and draws to six. This matches the official Nocturne rules. Fairgrounds previously used a ten-card pile; it now uses eight with two players and twelve with three or more, matching the official Cornucopia & Guilds rules.

Bauble’s existing choices now support a strategy hook, allowing the policy to choose a Favor when it wants to replenish Woodworkers’ Guild access. Strategies without a hook retain the existing coin-and-buy default.

Reproduce the experiments

Run these commands from the repository root. The search script records full strategy parameters and per-match seeds. Every shuffle seed is played with both starting seats; ties count as half a win.

PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_unused_cards_shepherd.py search --output .context/unused-cards-search.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_unused_cards_shepherd.py refine --input .context/unused-cards-search.json --output .context/unused-cards-refine.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_unused_cards_shepherd.py tournament --input .context/unused-cards-refine.json --output .context/unused-cards-tournament.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_unused_cards_shepherd.py validation --input .context/unused-cards-tournament.json --output .context/unused-cards-validation.json
PYTHONPATH=. python -m dominion.simulation.strategy_battle "Random Kingdom Shepherd Embassy Money" "Big Money" --board boards/random_unused_card_kingdom.txt --games 400 --workers 6

Commands above should each be entered on one line. The scripts use six worker processes by default; set --workers 1 for serial execution.
