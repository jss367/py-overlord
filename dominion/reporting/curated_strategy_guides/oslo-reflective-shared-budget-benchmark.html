October 14, 2026 · Five-seed benchmark

Oslo: Reflective Search with Equal Game Allowances

The study did not resolve a direct-matchup advantage across search runs.

Assisted recommendations won 1,352 of 2,000 fresh games against their genetic controls (67.6%). The 95% Student-t interval across five search-seed rates was 46.2% to 89.0%. Both arms received a 60,000-game search allowance per seed.

On the planned secondary measure, average opponent-panel wins rose from 58.2% to 74.0%. All 5 runs improved on that measure; the mean paired gain was 15.8 percentage points (95% seed-level interval: 9.5 to 22.2).

The reflective method, GEPA (Genetic-Pareto), used execution feedback to revise a validated JSON strategic plan. Both arms used the current genetic trainer with the expanded typed policy from the reference coverage audit enabled, alongside shared board-derived rule-list seeds. They began with the same board-derived plans and engine seeds; the assisted arm additionally received a selected reflective proposal with a distinct compiled rule/policy fingerprint in 5 of five runs. The audit's explicit reference encodings were not injected into search.

The hard cap covered reflective evaluations, genetic screening, refinement and confirmation, and recommendation selection. Both arms trained against the same fixed reference panel without adding hall-of-fame opponents. Thirty individuals and a ceiling of 30 generations were used. The trainer stopped before any generation whose full evaluation cycle and selection reserve could not fit.

After training, remaining games funded equal, complete seat pairs for every unique recommendation option and opponent within an arm. Thus selection sample sizes could differ between arms. Completed search expenditure ranged from 59,980 to 60,000 games; unused allowance ranged from 0 to 20 games, less than one complete selection pair block. The caps were equal; actual spending is reported rather than padded with discarded games.

The study played 621,870 simulator games, including 22,000 fixed-cost fresh games. GEPA used 7,200 search games and 10 Codex reflection requests, with 232862 input tokens and 12091 output tokens. Reflection used saved ChatGPT subscription sign-in; API-dollar estimates remain null. Equal game allowances do not equalize model computation or wall-clock time. Gameplay made no model calls.
