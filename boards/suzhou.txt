# Suzhou: a kingdom built so that Groundskeeper is the best card on the board.
#
# Groundskeeper scores a VP token for every Victory card gained while it is in
# play, so it wants three things: cheap Victory cards that do not clog the
# deck, ways to gain several of them per turn, and enough draw to play several
# Groundskeepers before those gains happen.
#
# Great Hall and Mill are cantrip Victory cards, so greening costs the deck no
# speed, and Crossroads draws one card per Victory card in hand, so the green
# cards feed the engine that gains more of them. Ironworks hands out Victory
# cards during the Action phase, after the Groundskeepers are already in play;
# Bridge and Market add the extra Buys (and the price break) that turn one turn
# into three or four Victory gains.
#
# Five Victory piles beyond Province -- Great Hall, Mill, Nobles, Estate and
# Duchy -- keep 40 Victory cards in reach of a deck that wants to gain them by
# the handful.
#
# Deliberately absent: Gardens, Silk Road and every other Victory card that
# pays for mass gaining by itself. On this board a gained Estate is worth one
# point plus one per Groundskeeper in play, so the tokens are the whole
# payload and a deck that skips Groundskeeper has nothing to gain them for.
#
# Two players; no Colony, Platinum, Potion, Shelters, or landscapes.
