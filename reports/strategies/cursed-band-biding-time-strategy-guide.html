Cursed Band • Biding Time • Baths

Take the curse. Keep the Loot.

This kingdom is not a conventional engine puzzle. Cursed Band of Misfits turns every $5 gain into flexible draw plus a $7 Loot. In two-player, the same rush can empty two Supply piles at once and set up an abrupt Province finish.

The verdict Rush Cursed Band of Misfits.

Open with Poet and Experiment, take Bands before building a cleaner engine, and use Biding Time to quarantine the junk you acquired along the way.

The core plan

One gain does four jobs

The Curse looks alarming, but the complete transaction is overwhelmingly favorable. The Loot supplies economy, Band supplies repeatable draw, and the Curse becomes far less intrusive once Biding Time is active.

1

Gain Cursed Band

Pay $5 or gain it with Altar when the pile is still live.

2

Collect a Loot

The $7 Treasure payload is the real economic engine.

3

Copy the right Action

Use Poet for Sun early; use Experiment for draw afterward.

4

Control the ending

In two-player, ten unshielded gains empty Band and Curse together.

Opening book

Buy the burst, not the infrastructure

Experiment is temporary, which is an advantage here: one purchase provides two early cantrip bursts without permanently swelling the deck. Poet starts the countdown to the Prophecy.

After shuffle

Gain priorities

What to buy

These priorities describe the build phase. Endgame math always wins: take the Province or pile-out when it secures the game, even if a build card appears higher.

  1. Cursed Band of MisfitsTake it aggressively while the pile is contested—even over an early Province when the game is not yet ending.
  2. ProvincePivot when the Band pile is nearly empty, the Province pile is vulnerable, or the opponent can end ahead.
  3. One King’s CacheAt $7, triple a Loot. One copy is enough; it is strongest after Loot is already circulating.
  4. Poet and ExperimentUsually one or two Poets and the opening Experiment pair. They activate and stabilize the plan.
  5. Altar, situationallyLate support that turns a Curse, Estate, or Copper into another Band. Do not delay the initial rush to buy it.
  6. Gold or late VictoryFallback gains after the defining piles are gone or the scoring pivot has begun.

Turn sequencing

Change masks at the right moment

Band’s flexibility is the tactical center of the deck. Its best target changes as soon as the fifth Sun token leaves Biding Time in a two-player game.

Before Biding Time: copy Poet

Remove Sun tokens when activation is close enough to affect this cleanup or your next hand.

After activation: copy Experiment

Band becomes reliable +2 Cards and +1 Action without returning the Band itself to its pile.

Then resolve terminal payloads

Play Altar or Imperial Envoy only after cantrips and Bands have supplied enough actions and cards.

Leave dead cards in hand

Unplayed Estates and Curses return next turn with the new five-card hand instead of entering the shuffle.

Use a Baths banking turn

If you cannot gain Band or Province, play and gain nothing: take 2 victory points and begin next turn with roughly ten cards.

Do not bank a winning hand

Never pass a critical Band, Province, or favorable pile-out merely to collect 2 victory points.

Card-by-card guidance

Know what not to buy

Most of the kingdom is slower than the defining interaction. These cards have roles, but they should not distract from the Band race.

Buy one

King’s Cache

Tripling a Loot creates explosive money and often extra buys or gains. Hold it under Biding Time when necessary to ensure the collision.

Later

Altar

Excellent when it gains another Cursed Band, but paying $6 for Altar instead of taking an available Band usually loses tempo.

Multiplayer

Trader

It can replace the Curse from a Band gain with Silver. That is attractive when the Curse pile will not empty with Band.

Usually skip

Trading Post

Slow, terminal cleanup is unnecessary once Biding Time can keep dead cards out of the shuffle.

Usually skip

Cursed Village

The Hex is real and its draw is redundant beside Band-as-Experiment, Poet, and Biding Time.

Usually skip

Imperial Envoy

Five cards and +1 Buy are powerful, but the terminal stop and 2 Debt are worse than racing the defining $5 pile.

Player-count adjustment

Trader changes the ending

Trader’s reaction is not automatically upside. Replacing the Curse also leaves that Curse in its pile, which can remove the two-pile ending from the strategy.

Two players

Usually skip Trader. Ten unshielded Cursed Band gains can empty the ten-card Band pile and ten-card Curse pile together. Aim to make Province the third pile.

Three or more players

Consider one Trader after the opening. The larger Curse pile will not empty alongside Band, so preserving Trader under Biding Time as a reaction shield is more attractive.

Simulation evidence

The rush survived comparison

Repository simulations were used to compare broad archetypes and refine card priorities. They validate the direction of the plan, not perfect human play.

The Cursed Band rush won 90 of the initial 100 games against a Poet, Experiment, Ghost Town, and Imperial Envoy engine.

Modeling note: the simulator is most useful here for structural comparisons. Human players should calculate pile endings, choose Band’s target tactically, and override any fixed priority when a Province or final gain wins.
