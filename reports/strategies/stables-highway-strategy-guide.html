Assumptions: two players, seven Coppers and three Estates each, eight Provinces, ten Curses, and no landscapes, Shelters, Platinum or Colony.

The best plan found

Buy two Margraves, then money and points. Keep buying Silver when you cannot afford Gold or Province. Two copies increase how often you find Margrave. This deck normally plays one per turn; it does not build an Inn engine to play both.

Open the runnable strategy and its exact priorities.

  1. Open Silver/Silver on 4/3. On 5/2, buy Margrave on the $5 turn and pass on the $2 turn.
  2. Take Province whenever affordable. There is no delayed greening threshold.
  3. Get the second Margrave before additional treasure upgrades. Once you own two, buy Gold at $6–$7 and Silver at $3–$5, subject to the scoring rules below. There is no practical Silver cap: the search limit is 99 copies, above the supply.
  4. Buy Duchies with four or fewer Provinces remaining. Duchy then outranks another Margrave or Gold. Add Estates with one or fewer Provinces remaining, after Province and Duchy when those are affordable.
  5. Use Margrave’s extra buy. A $11 turn can buy Province plus Silver. A $13 turn can buy Province plus Duchy once the Duchy threshold is reached.

Why this beats the elaborate builds in the search

Margrave supplies draw, an extra buy and a hand-size attack in one purchase. Extra Silver keeps the reduced hands productive. Inn is the board’s only village, and its compulsory discard makes a large Stables–Highway deck demanding to assemble. The tested engines spent too many purchases on support before scoring.

Weaver has a real counterplay role: discard it to Margrave and gain two Silvers without spending an Action on your turn. Its best tested plans still finished below the uncapped-Silver Margrave decks. Guard Dog filters the hand before Margrave’s attack, but the attack still reduces that hand to three. Witch’s Hut requires two Action discards to give a Curse; its tested Inn/Weaver plans did not catch the money leaders.

What the comparisons say

The broad screen played 181 parameter sets against six opponents (26,064 games). Joint refinement evaluated 221 entries (47,736 games); clean alternative families added 46 entries (8,832 games); and Weaver refinement evaluated 24 entries (6,144 games). Entries can overlap across stages.

The final round robin had 23 entrants, 253 pairings and 120 games per pairing, split equally between seats: 30,360 games. Two-Margrave money with Duchies at four Provinces led at 68.6%; one-Margrave money was second at 67.2%. The next two finishes were two-Margrave money with a later Duchy threshold (66.2%) and one-Margrave money with that later threshold (66.1%).

The winner was fixed before another 10,000 fresh validation games: 1,000 against each opponent below. Across all 129,136 formal games, no game hit the 160-round safeguard. Validation had no ties.

The broad finding is stronger than the fine tuning. Margrave money beat the tested engine and reaction alternatives clearly. Its 52.1% against one Margrave, 50.8% against later Duchies, and 51.5% against earlier Duchies do not establish a clear advantage: all three intervals include 50%. Two Margraves and the four-Province Duchy threshold remain the frozen point-estimate recommendation; one Margrave is a credible simpler alternative.

Against the one-Margrave opponent, the recommended deck averaged 6.8 Silvers, 3.1 Golds, two Margraves and 4.1 Provinces at the end. The game finished around round 20.6. These are final deck averages, rather than construction targets.

How much confidence to place in this

This is the best policy found in a bounded search, rather than a proof of optimal play. The exact second-Margrave and Duchy thresholds need particular caution: nearby money policies had close round-robin scores. The 23 finalists are parameterized entrants; different targets can sometimes lead to the same purchases when an Action limit prevents a later target.

The search varied copy targets, treasure density, greening, terminal allowance, opening preferences, purchase order, and free-gain choices. It included clean synergistic families and joint mutations on a second search seed. It did not optimize every tactical decision or conditional response to an opponent’s deck. Results apply to this two-player setup.

Validation intervals below are approximate 95% intervals, using each same-seed, seat-swapped pair as one independent observation. Ties count as half a win. Intervals are not adjusted for multiple comparisons. Screening panel scores are only comparable within their stage.

Rules checked before the search

Compared with the publisher’s Hinterlands rulebook, the simulator needed several corrections. Inn now discards exactly two cards. Inn, Margrave and Witch’s Hut accept player-selected simultaneous discards, enabling Weaver reactions and deliberate two-Action discards. Weaver and Develop use discounted costs; free gains have priorities separate from purchases. Haggler’s gain is mandatory and respects discounts, including the zero-cost floor. Guard Dog can respond even when Witch’s Hut gives no Curse.

Cartographer’s default filtering, Inn’s choice to shuffle all discarded Actions, and Develop’s cheaper-card-first gain order remain fixed legal policies.

Reproduce the search

Run from the repository root. Every match records its parameters and seed; each shuffle seed is played from both seats. The winner and validation opponents were fixed before validation.

PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_stables_highway.py screen \
  --games 24 --workers 6 --output .context/stables-highway-screen-corrected.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_stables_highway.py refine \
  --input .context/stables-highway-screen-corrected.json --games 24 \
  --seed 20261021 --workers 8 --output .context/stables-highway-refine.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_stables_highway.py alternatives \
  --input .context/stables-highway-screen-corrected.json --games 24 \
  --workers 4 --output .context/stables-highway-alternatives.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_stables_highway.py weaver \
  --input .context/stables-highway-screen-corrected.json --games 32 \
  --workers 4 --output .context/stables-highway-weaver.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_stables_highway.py tournament \
  --input .context/stables-highway-refine.json \
  --extra .context/stables-highway-alternatives.json .context/stables-highway-weaver.json \
  --games 120 --finalists 6 --workers 8 --output .context/stables-highway-tournament.json
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/search_stables_highway.py validate \
  --input scripts/data/stables_highway_selection.json --games 1000 \
  --workers 8 --output .context/stables-highway-validation.json
